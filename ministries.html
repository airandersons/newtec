Ministry Spotlight: Community Outreach

Transforming Kampala One Life at a Time

Our community outreach ministry serves the vulnerable in Kampala through:

  • Monthly food distribution to 200+ families
  • School fee assistance for orphaned children
  • Medical camps in slum communities
  • Skills training for unemployed youth
  • Prison ministry and rehabilitation support
500+ Lives touched monthly
15 Community partners
