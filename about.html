Our Journey Through the Years

From humble beginnings to a vibrant city church

1996

The Beginning

A small group of 15 believers began meeting in a rented room in Kitintale, led by Bishop Peter Mutebi. With a vision to establish a Christ-centered, Bible-believing church in Kampala.

1998

First Permanent Home

After five years of growth, we purchased our first property on Gospel Street. The congregation had grown to over 200 members, and we began our community outreach programs.

2005

Expanding Our Reach

We launched our first cell group ministry, establishing 5 home fellowships across Kampala. This marked our commitment to community-based discipleship.

2012

New Sanctuary Dedication

After years of planning and construction, we dedicated our current 800-seat sanctuary. The church had grown to over 500 regular attendees with multiple Sunday services.

2020-Present

Digital Transformation & Continued Growth

During the pandemic, we launched our online ministry, reaching thousands worldwide. Today, we have 25+ cell groups, multiple ministries, and continue to impact Kampala for Christ.

What We Believe

Our foundational beliefs based on the Bible

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our final authority in all matters of faith and practice.

Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, sinless life, miracles, atoning death, bodily resurrection, and imminent return.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present ministry of the Holy Spirit, who indwells every believer and empowers us for godly living and service.

The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, called to worship God, preach the Gospel, and make disciples of all nations.

Salvation

We believe salvation comes by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, not by works, and is available to all who believe.

Great Commission

We believe every Christian is called to share the Gospel and make disciples, starting in our local community of Kampala.
