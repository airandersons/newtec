Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about studying at VBI

Is VBI accredited?

Yes, Victory Bible Institute is accredited by the Uganda National Council for Higher Education and recognized by several international theological accrediting bodies.

What are the class schedules like?

We offer flexible schedules including evening classes (6-9 PM) on weekdays and Saturday morning sessions to accommodate working professionals and active church members.

Can I study online or part-time?

We currently offer in-person classes with plans to launch hybrid options. Part-time study is available for certificate and diploma programs.

What career paths do graduates pursue?

Graduates serve as pastors, missionaries, Christian educators, counselors, worship leaders, church administrators, and in various NGO roles.
