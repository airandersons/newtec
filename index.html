A Living Church in the Heart of Kampala

For over 30 years, we've been a beacon of hope and transformation in our city

Welcome to New Testament Evangelical Church, where faith comes alive and lives are transformed through the power of the Gospel. Located in the vibrant heart of Kampala, we're more than just a church—we're a family.

For three decades, we've been committed to preaching Christ crucified and risen, creating a space where everyone can encounter God's love and grow in their faith journey.
