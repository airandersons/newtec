Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about cell fellowships

What happens at a typical cell meeting?

Meetings typically include fellowship time, worship, Bible study/discussion, prayer for one another, and planning for outreach or service projects. Each group has its own rhythm but follows this general pattern.

Do I need to be a member of NTEC to join?

Not at all! Our cell fellowships are open to everyone - regular attenders, new visitors, and even those just exploring faith. It's a great way to get to know our church community in a smaller setting.

How often do cell groups meet?

Most groups meet weekly, though some may meet bi-weekly. The specific schedule is listed with each fellowship. Consistency helps build strong relationships.

Can I visit a group before committing?

Absolutely! We encourage visitors to attend 2-3 meetings to get a feel for the group dynamics and see if it's a good fit before making any commitment.
